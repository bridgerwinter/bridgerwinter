Smashlight: Brick Breaker — Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026 · BW Services and Development LLC

In short

Smashlight has no accounts and we don't collect your name, email or contacts. Your progress stays on your device. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device data to show and measure those ads. Purchases are handled by Apple or Google; we never see your payment details.

Who we are

Smashlight: Brick Breaker ("Smashlight", "the app") is made by BW Services and Development LLC, Hawaii, USA ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles on iOS and Android.

Information we do not collect

We don't ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or account details. There is no sign-in, and we run no servers that receive data from the app.

Information stored on your device

Your scores, coins, unlocked levels and items, upgrade progress, daily-challenge results and settings (sound, vibration) are saved only on your device. We can't see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner, full-screen and optional rewarded video ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android), device type and settings, app information, IP address and approximate location derived from it, ad interactions, and crash and performance diagnostics. Google uses this to deliver and measure ads, limit how often you see them, and prevent fraud, and may use it to personalize ads where you allow it.

How Google uses this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices about ads

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland, and where required). The app asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the "Privacy choices" link on the app's main menu.
  • Tracking on iOS. On iPhone and iPad, the app only uses your advertising identifier for tracking if you allow it in Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can change this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Android. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads).
  • Remove Ads. The one-time "Remove Ads" purchase turns off ads in the app.

Purchases

The "Remove Ads" purchase is processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play under their terms and privacy policies. We receive only a confirmation that the purchase was made, so the app can turn ads off and restore the purchase on reinstall. We never receive your payment card or billing details.

Sharing your score

The Daily challenge's "Share" button copies a short text summary of your result to your device's clipboard. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you paste it yourself.

Children

Smashlight is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we'll help remove it.

How long data is kept and how to delete it

Game data on your device stays until you delete the app. We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Data collected by Google for advertising is kept and deleted under Google's policies; you can reset your advertising identifier as described above.

Security

Because we don't collect or store your personal information on our own systems, there is no account or server of ours to breach. Ads and purchases use Google's and Apple's own secure systems.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. We don't sell personal information. To exercise a right, contact us below; for advertising data you can also contact Google directly.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
