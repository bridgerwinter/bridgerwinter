When you're billed
Your first month is charged when you sign up, then on the same date each month.
Receipts
Every payment comes with an emailed receipt for your records.
Plan terms
12-month plans have $0 setup and continue month-to-month after the first year. Canceling before 12 months? A $299 early-cancellation fee applies. Starter is also available month-to-month with a $299 setup fee.
Edits and extra work
Each plan's monthly edit time is used that month and doesn't roll over. Work beyond it is $75/hour, always quoted before I start.
Custom projects
The build is quoted after a free chat. The monthly plan starts at $249 and depends on what your custom tool needs to run.
Tax
Prices are plus Hawaiʻi General Excise Tax (GET), shown on every invoice.
